Why this audio is difficult

  • Panel format, so two people frequently talk over each other
  • Studio stings and background music between segments
  • Guests joining by phone at much lower bandwidth
  • Heavy code switching: English finance terms inside Bangla sentences, for example রেভিনিউ and ফিসকাল পলিসি
  • Episodes run 24 to 48 minutes, far past what a speech model reads in one pass

02 / ARCHITECTURE

Five stages, each writing output the next one reads

Nothing is held in memory between stages. Every stage writes durable structured output, so any single stage can be rerun, audited or replaced without touching the ones before it.

Gain is applied selectively, not to everything

An episode is changed only when it is genuinely off target and the true peak leaves headroom. Roughly one third of measured episodes needed adjustment and the rest were left alone. Normalising everything blindly would clip the loud ones and introduce distortion the speech models would then have to work around.

Why this matters downstream

Diarization clusters speaker embeddings by distance. If one episode arrives 12 dB quieter than another, the embeddings shift and a clustering threshold that worked on one recording stops working on the next. Fixing loudness first is what lets a single threshold setting apply across the whole corpus.

Loudness before and after is recorded per episode, and every model is pinned to an exact revision, so the run can be repeated and compared rather than only described.

What the post-processing is for

  • Segments under 0.75 s are dropped, so a cough or a jingle does not become a speaker
  • Gaps under 0.17 s are stitched, so one sentence does not split into three turns
  • Any speaker with under 9 s of total airtime is deleted, which removes spurious clusters
  • Both the raw and the post-processed output are kept, so the effect of each threshold can be inspected rather than assumed

No diarization error rate is reported. That metric needs a human made reference of who spoke when, and none exists for this corpus. Scoring the output against itself would be meaningless, so the number is left blank rather than estimated.

Why each setting is set that way

  • 28 s rather than 30 s leaves headroom inside the receptive field, so the window edge never lands exactly at the model limit
  • Language forced to Bengali stops the detector switching to Hindi or Assamese on a noisy chunk
  • No conditioning on previous text stops a hallucination in one chunk being fed as context into the next, which is how one error becomes a whole bad minute
  • Source separation on detection measures spectral flux per chunk and separates music only when it is actually present, rather than degrading clean speech

A Bengali adapted checkpoint matters for the financial vocabulary. It produces মূল্যস্ফীতি, খেলাপি ঋণ and বৈদেশিক মুদ্রার রিজার্ভ as clean tokens rather than phonetic approximations, and the whole downstream lexicon depends on that.

06
06 / TRANSCRIPT QUALITY

Two defects, found by reading the output rather than trusting it

Chunk boundary truncation 400 of 3,532

Bangla never begins a word with a dependent vowel sign. When a segment does, the stitcher has eaten the opening consonant. That makes this a mechanical test, not a judgement call.

ুক্তা উন্নতি লক্ষ্য করা যাচ্ছে

The word should be কিছুটা. The first token of 11.3 percent of utterances is not a real word.

Fix

Chunks are currently cut back to back with no overlap. Cutting them with a 2 to 3 second overlap and stitching on the shared region removes the boundary word entirely. This is a rerun setting, not new code.

Decoder repetition loops 46 of 3,532

The decoder locks onto one token and emits it for many seconds. Detected by the longest run of a repeated token, and by lexical variety across a long span.

বাজার বাজার বাজার বাজার বাজার বাজার

These carry no readable meaning, so they are dropped from supervision rather than quietly labelled neutral.

Likely cause, and it is in the configuration

The run sets a repetition penalty of 0.8. A penalty above 1.0 suppresses tokens already produced. A value below 1.0 does the opposite and makes repeating them more likely. Raising it above 1.0, or adding a no repeat constraint, is the direct fix.

Neither defect appeared in any summary report. Both were found by printing actual segments and reading them, which is the argument for looking at your data: the pipeline reported success at every stage while writing 400 broken first words.

07 / STAGE 4, FUSION

Attaching every word to a speaker

Diarization gives time intervals with speaker labels. Speech recognition gives words with timestamps. Neither knows about the other, so fusion decides which speaker owns each word.

The assignment rule

  • Take the midpoint of the word. If it falls inside one speaker interval, that speaker owns the word
  • If the midpoint falls in a gap, fall back to the interval with the largest overlap
  • If two speakers overlap in time, the one who started first keeps the word, so no word is counted twice
  • Consecutive words from the same speaker are grouped into an utterance, which becomes the unit everything downstream works on

The midpoint is used rather than the word start because start timestamps drift at chunk boundaries and the midpoint is the more stable of the two.

Finding what a sentence is actually about

Sentiment is meaningless without a target. Before polarity can be assigned, the system finds the financial thing being discussed, and Bangla morphology makes that harder than a string match.

09 / THE POLARITY IDEA

A rising number is not good or bad until you know what is rising

This is the decision that makes the task financial rather than generic. Ordinary sentiment analysis treats an increase word as positive. In economic talk that is wrong about half the time.

বৈদেশিক মুদ্রার রিজার্ভ বেড়েছে

Foreign exchange reserves have increased.

Reserves carry orientation +1. The increase cue keeps its sign. Result positive

মূল্যস্ফীতি বেড়েছে

Inflation has increased.

Inflation carries orientation -1. The same cue flips. Result negative

Orientation +1

14 targets where a rise is good news: reserves, remittance, GDP, exports, investment, employment, revenue, output, purchasing power, the market index.

Orientation -1

9 targets where a rise is bad news: inflation, consumer prices, bad loans, foreign debt, capital flight, the policy rate, public spending, imports.

Orientation 0

29 entity targets such as banks, the government or the energy sector. A rise means nothing on its own, so these are scored only by evaluative words.

Because each target is scored separately, one sentence can carry two labels at once. In this corpus a single utterance is negative about inflation and positive about reserves in the same breath. A sentence level model cannot represent that.

10 / SUPERVISION

Creating labels that can be traced back to words

A written rule set is used rather than a black box, so every training label can be explained by the exact tokens that produced it.

scoring one target in one utterance 
# every cue token within 12 tokens of the target
polarity = cue.polarity
# direction words depend on the target
if cue.kind == "direction":
    if orientation == 0: skip
    polarity *= orientation
# adjectives and verbs can be negated, nouns cannot
if cue.negatable and negator within 3 tokens:
    polarity *= -1
# nearer evidence counts for more
decay = 1 / (1 + distance / 4)
score += polarity * cue.weight * decay

Above +0.55 is positive, below -0.55 is negative, anything between is non evaluative. The cue set holds 104 stems, each with a weight and a negation flag.

Two corrections found by reading the output

Negation has scope. A first version flipped any cue near a negator and scored crisis as positive.

সংকট কাটছে না

The crisis is not passing. The না negates the verb, not the noun, and it is still bad news. Only adjectives and verbs are now marked negatable.

Bangla builds a negated verb complex. The particle can sit three tokens from the word it negates.

স্থিতিশীল করতে পারবেন না

You will not be able to stabilise it. Scored positive until the negation window was widened to three.

11 / SPLIT

Split by episode, never by row

Two utterances from the same talk show share the panel, the topic, the host's phrasing and the same recognition error pattern. If rows from one episode land in both training and test, the model can recognise the episode instead of the sentiment, and the score will not repeat on a new recording.

So all four test episodes are held out whole. They were chosen to carry as much of the hand read reference set as possible, which is why the final number is measured on human labels from recordings the model never saw.

The split asserts that no episode appears in more than one partition, so a careless edit fails loudly instead of quietly inflating the score.

Validation is used only to pick the stopping epoch and is never fitted on. Because the reference slice inside validation is small, model selection uses the rule label agreement score, which is the honest consequence of having limited human labels.

Why not a random row split

A random split over 3,019 rows would place utterances from the same recording on both sides. The reported score would rise, and none of that rise would survive contact with a new episode. The episode split is the harder and the honest measurement.

Preprocessing and label creation

transcript defect detection 
# A word may never begin with a dependent sign.
# When a segment does, the stitcher ate a consonant.
def leading_truncated(text):
    s = text.lstrip()
    return bool(s) and s[0] in COMBINING

# Longest run of one token, plus lexical variety.
# The run catches a single repeated token, the
# ratio catches an alternating loop.
def repetition_score(text):
    t = text.split()
    longest = run = 1
    for i in range(1, len(t)):
        run = run + 1 if t[i] == t[i-1] else 1
        longest = max(longest, run)
    return longest, len(set(t)) / len(t)

is_loop = (longest >= 4
           or (words >= 20 and ratio < 0.35))
target aware polarity scoring 
for i, (_, _, token) in enumerate(spans):
    distance = min(abs(i - t) for t in target_idx)
    if distance > WINDOW:
        continue
    ...
    polarity = cue["polarity"]
    if cue["kind"] == "direction":
        # A rise is only good or bad
        # relative to what is rising.
        if orientation == 0:
            continue
        polarity *= orientation

    # Negation flips only what can be negated,
    # and only when the particle is adjacent.
    negated = False
    if cue["negatable"]:
        tail = {spans[j][2] for j in
                range(i + 1, min(i + 4, len(spans)))}
        negated = bool(tail & NEGATORS)
        if negated:
            polarity *= -1

    decay = 1.0 / (1.0 + distance / 4.0)
    score += polarity * cue["weight"] * decay

Rows flagged as repetition loops, and rows under six words, are excluded from supervision rather than silently labelled neutral. That is 90 of 3,109 pairs.

14 / METRICS

Which numbers are reported, and why those

The class balance decides the metric. With 85.6 percent of rows in one class, the obvious metric is actively misleading, so it is not used as the headline.

Plain accuracy, rejected

A model that answers non evaluative every single time scores 85.6 percent on this corpus and has learned nothing. Accuracy is reported next to the others for completeness, never on its own.

Macro F1, the headline

Averages F1 over the three classes with equal weight, so the 152 positive rows count as much as the 2,583 neutral ones. The all neutral model scores 0.31 here, which is what makes the metric useful.

Per class precision and recall

Macro F1 hides which class is broken. Splitting it out is what revealed that positive precision is the real weakness, at 0.417 across the full reference set.

Cohen kappa

Agreement corrected for what two raters would hit by chance alone. On the 180 reference pairs the rule set reaches 0.392, which is fair agreement, not good agreement. Raw agreement of 59.4 percent would sound far better than it is.

Confusion matrix

The only view that says which direction the errors run. A single F1 cannot tell a model that misses negatives apart from one that invents positives. Here it showed both, in very different amounts.

Deliberately not reported: word error rate, diarization error rate, and speaker attribution accuracy. Each needs a human made reference, and this project has none. Word to speaker coverage is reported instead, and is clearly labelled as coverage rather than accuracy.

The structural cause

Class imbalance. 85.6 percent of rows are non evaluative and only 91 positive examples reach training. That is far too few for a 110 million parameter model, and thin even for logistic regression.

Largest: advice read as praise

24 errors. A speaker says what should happen, and the rule set counts the good word as a judgement about the present.

অর্থনীতি স্থিতিশীল করতে হবে

The economy must be stabilised. A demand, not a compliment.

Negation carried by a suffix

Bangla negates the past tense by attaching নি to the verb, so there is no separate particle to find.

বাজেট বাস্তবায়ন হয়নি

The budget was not implemented. The system sees implementation and answers positive.

Negative meaning, no cue word

22 errors. The cue set holds 104 stems. Plain Bangla carries bad news without using any of them.

17 / IMPROVING IT

Ranked by expected gain per hour of work

1
Label 800 to 1,000 pairs by hand, with two annotators

The single biggest lever, and it removes the ceiling rather than raising it. Training on real labels instead of rule output means the model is no longer bounded by the teacher. Two annotators also give an agreement score on the labels themselves. Six members at roughly 150 pairs each is one working session.

Highest gain
2
Correct the two recognition settings

Raise the repetition penalty above 1.0, or add a no repeat constraint, to stop the decoder loops. Cut the 28 second chunks with a 2 to 3 second overlap and stitch on the shared region, which removes the truncated first word from 11.3 percent of utterances. Both are configuration changes that clean the input for every later stage.

Upstream
3
Separate prescription from evaluation

The largest single error class, 24 of 73. Bangla marks obligation with a small closed set of endings, chiefly করতে হবে, উচিত, দরকার and প্রয়োজন. Detecting those and suppressing the polarity claim is a short rule, not a research problem.

Cheap and large
4
Handle the suffix negation

Matching a verb final নি catches a class of sign flips that currently go straight into the training labels, because the negation window has no separate token to see.

Cheap
5
Train the sentence pair model, then widen the corpus

The training code is written and the checkpoint loads. It reads whole phrases rather than character n-grams, so it can learn that a fall in investment is bad without that phrase sitting in a cue list. Worth running once the real labels exist. After that, 26 episodes is simply a small corpus, and the pipeline runs end to end on new material without a code change.
